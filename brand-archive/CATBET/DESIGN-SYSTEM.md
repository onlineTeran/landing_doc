## 3. Mascot system

### Immutable until brand approval

- canonical cyan/blue fur color;
- orange/red eyes and orange collar;
- species, face, body shape і proportions;
- узнаваність навіть без logo;
- відсутність bodybuilder/muscular deformation.
- no clothes by default.

### Potentially adaptable

- pose, expression, camera, approved role-specific clothing/props;
- interaction with product/host character;
- animation, якщо identity frames лишаються canonical.

### Forbidden by default

- інший base color або форма;
- generic blue-cat substitute;
- aggression/childishness without concept approval;
- costume, що змінює identity або порушує compliance.

## 4. Material grammar

- Furry tactile cyan/orange surfaces for brand-specific titles, numbers and selected utility objects.
- Soft/furry icon family uses consistent fiber length, density, camera, light and shadow footprint.
- CatBoxes follow canonical tier/package form; open/closed state must match mechanics.
- Duck, fish, paw, yarn and gift motifs are secondary narrative vocabulary.
- Concrete is not CATBET-native. In a BETON bridge it is matte, gray, porous and realistically cracked;
  CATBET cyan/orange glow may live inside selected cracks but not turn stone into glossy toy plastic.

## 5. Composition grammar

- One signature scene/visual metaphor per page.
- Reward hierarchy: main numeric/reward moment dominates, supporting benefits stay subordinate.
- Logo supports recognition but does not replace mascot/material/typography.
- Asset families use matched optical weight, not identical bounding-box size.
- Avoid generic casino neon, glassmorphism and unrelated bordered cards.

## 6. CTA and host bridge

For same-brand CATBET, extract CTA family from current CATBET design system before G3.

For cross-brand:

- host normally owns button shape, states, spacing and UI typography;
- CATBET owns offer language and visual anchors;
- destination color must not silently restyle the host CTA;
- exact ownership is written in Brand Bridge.

## 7. Motion vocabulary

- CATBET motion should express tactile bounce, pounce, paw/fur reaction or narrative continuity.
- One dominant motion per viewport; ambient motion stays subtle.
- Global reveal-on-scroll is off by default.
- Hero video needs approved still, safe zones, `contain/cover` contract and reduced-motion poster.
- Motion cannot carry a mechanic or legal qualifier alone.

## 8. Design QA signature

A CATBET design fails if swapping logo makes it look like any casino. Approval requires at least three
independent CATBET signals from canonical sources: for example mascot identity + material family +
typography/offer object. A large logo counts as only one signal.
