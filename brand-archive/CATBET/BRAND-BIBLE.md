## 1. Brand core

- CATBET is a character brand, not a campaign wrapper.
- Luck is treated as a territory and a sensed moment, not as a guaranteed outcome.
- The central idea is **«Злови удачу за хвіст.»** It describes the emotional territory; it must not be
  rewritten into a promise of winning.
- The universe is built around feline intuition polished over millennia: ancient people observed cats,
  then CATBET turned that intuition into its own casino world.
- Inside the product, Bet is the host/owner and singular voice, not a support agent or corporate team.

## 2. Emotional territory

| CATBET is | CATBET is not |
|---|---|
| «тут завжди є хтось, хто знає більше» | «зараз або ніколи» |
| trust in the cat's intuition | FOMO and pressure |
| feeling that this is the right moment/place | promise that chance can be controlled |
| calm anticipation and a precise cue to act | aggressive excitement at any cost |

The brand may dramatize a moment, but cannot imply that Bet guarantees a win or knows a random result.

## 3. Brand personality

- confident without arrogance;
- calm because he controls the situation, not because he is indifferent;
- brief and to the point;
- witty, occasionally teasing, never toxic;
- never fusses, begs or repeats;
- warm without flattery;
- cunning and quick-witted — always lands on his paws;
- slightly dramatic and fond of a memorable entrance.

See [MASCOT.md](MASCOT.md) for the executable identity and pose contract.

## 4. Communication behavior

Bet's appearance is itself a message: luck is near; pay attention. He does not over-explain or hard-sell.
Default communication uses Bet's first-person voice or a direct feline cue. Copy is short enough to land
with one glance and the visual carries one action.

### Preferred behavior

- one line, one action, one message;
- direct verbs and concrete nouns;
- short tease or dry observation;
- cat vocabulary only when it clarifies the idea: tail, paw, purr, mice, instinct;
- human warmth without pseudo-friendship or exaggerated praise;
- clear CTA and literal conditions near any offer.

### Forbidden behavior

- shouting casino clichés, all-caps pressure, timers and «ОСТАННІЙ ШАНС»;
- «ми продаємо», empty promises or pretending the brand controls randomness;
- childlike cuteness, meme spam or a twitching mascot;
- aggressive gambling, panic, shame or coercion;
- dense copy that explains the joke after Bet has already made it.

## 5. Visual grammar

1. **Bet is the subject.** He acts, watches or controls; he is not decoration.
2. **One hero, one action, one message.** Avoid multiple equal focal points.
3. **Background is context.** It should read immediately and never become noise.
4. **Paw and tail are functional signs.** A paw may point; a tail may carry the story.
5. **Cyan + orange establish ownership.** Black/dark surfaces give the character and typography space;
   exact token values must come from editable source, never a raster eyedropper.
6. **Display type is bold and compact.** Headlines are large; body copy remains literal and readable.
7. **People are not main heroes.** If present, they support Bet's story.
8. **No generic casino shorthand.** No random coins, flying banknotes, giant jackpot faces or visual clutter.
9. **Logo is a separate exact layer.** Never regenerate or spell it with an image model.

## 6. Mascot appearance

- blue/cyan adult short-haired cat;
- orange/red eyes and orange collar;
- realistic tactile fur and natural cat anatomy;
- default: no clothes;
- clothing/props only for an explicit role and never as identity replacement;
- calm, knowing gaze; playful or active when the story requires it;
- collar remains visible and orange.

## 8. Creative format system

### Static banner

Bet occupies the frame and acts or observes. His gaze has intent. Copy is Bet's short line; logo and CTA
are exact layout elements. The frame should hook, intrigue and provoke action without pressure.

### Animated banner

- 3–6 seconds;
- one feline action, one message;
- motion is fluid and physical, never a chaotic effect stack;
- the cat finds the moment; the bonus/reward does not become an invented certainty;
- optional sound: purr as ambient layer, one short UI accent, no loud casino loop.

### Video 6 / 15 / 30 seconds

- 6s: one action + one message, no introduction;
- 15s: micro-story with one clear turning point and feline reaction;
- 30s: complete story where Bet/cat remains the subject; human character only when the narrative
  genuinely requires it;
- tone stays light, confident and character-led.

### In-app and push

- the speaker is Bet or a named cat character, never an anonymous system;
- one line describing what happened plus one feline remark;
- push: one sentence, no pressure; Bet has already acted or noticed something before the user opens it.

## 9. Artifacts and reusable motifs

- orange collar — immutable identity marker;
- paw — attention/gesture/pointer;
- tail — story continuity and catch metaphor;
- yarn — pursuit/play narrative;
- mice, paw token, nine-lives heart, yin-yang/feng-shui and Catback loop — lore artifacts whose product
  meaning must be separately validated;
- tactile fur may extend into icons or display art only from an approved style lock.

## 10. QA questions

- Is Bet still recognizable with the logo hidden?
- Is he the subject rather than decoration?
- Is the default no-clothes identity respected, with the orange collar visible?
- Does the frame contain one action and one message?
- Could the copy be read as a guarantee or pressure tactic?
- Are mechanic, reward and CTA claims backed by Product Truth?
- Are exact logo/text layers separate from generated raster?
- Does mobile keep face, eyes, collar, CTA and legal safe zones?

If any answer fails, the asset returns to the current ART phase; it does not advance by adding a larger logo.
