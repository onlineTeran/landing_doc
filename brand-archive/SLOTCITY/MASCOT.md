# SlotCity Mascot Evidence

**Design System version:** `0.9` · **Status:** `LIVE IDENTITY REFERENCE / PASSPORT PENDING`

The current `/levels` page supplies the first local, dated production reference for the SlotCity city
mascot. It improves generation quality but does not close the missing owner-approved mascot passport.

## Observed identity lock

- tall faceless urban figure; no visible face, skin, eyes or mouth;
- body fully enclosed by flowing white draped cloth with a soft rounded head silhouette;
- black oval/rounded sunglasses placed over the cloth;
- yellow headwear and oversized yellow high-top shoes;
- white gloves and expressive long-limbed movement;
- thick gold chain/pendant language;
- charismatic street/city energy, playful confidence rather than horror.

## Current environment lock

The `/levels` reference places the figure in a modern metropolitan SlotCity world: monumental city
scale, roads/transport, tall architecture, warm yellow-gold accents and cinematic wide-angle motion.
The saved OG snapshot adds a night entertainment-city reference. Treat these as separate roles in a
generation prompt: `identity` and `environment`.

## Supporting character evidence

The owner-supplied cross-brand ensemble additionally shows two supporting SlotCity characters:

- a red-haired woman with shamrock/green luck styling and gold jewelry;
- an elegant blue genie woman with a jeweled turban, blue skin and gold/jewel details.

Their visual presence is evidence, but canonical names, lore, anatomy variants and modification rights
have not been supplied. Use the exact bridge source/crops from
[`../BRIDGES/SLOTCITY-CATBET/README.md`](../BRIDGES/SLOTCITY-CATBET/README.md); do not invent names or
promote them above the main city mascot without an Art Brief decision.

## Forbidden inference

- do not expose or invent a face under the cloth;
- do not turn the figure into a skull, horror ghost, religious figure or generic sheet costume;
- do not recolor the white/yellow/black identity combination without approval;
- do not remove defining sunglasses/headwear/shoes/chain simultaneously;
- do not call pose, outfit detail, anatomy, name or personality canonical beyond what the reference shows;
- do not bake or regenerate the SlotCity logo when an exact SVG layer is available.

## Pose evidence

The evidence pack now contains both a dynamic city pose and a relaxed leaning pose. The relaxed pose
uses one elbow on a supporting object/railing, crossed ankles and one casual hand gesture. Treat the
social-media objects in its source as composition context only: never reproduce third-party marks unless
the Art Brief explicitly requires and licenses them.
