## Anti-invention rules

- No color extraction from third-party compressed screenshots as canonical tokens.
- No CATBET furry objects, paws, CatBoxes or vocabulary unless a Brand Bridge assigns CATBET a role.
- No default neon/glass/casino-symbol style without SlotCity evidence.
- No generic CTA substituted for the production component.
- No concept passes G3 until exact sources are attached.

## Definition of complete

All rows have source, extracted rule, owner and last-verified date; at least one 1440 and one 430/375
reference frame demonstrate the system in context; a brand owner approves the snapshot.
