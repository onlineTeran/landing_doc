# PlayCity / gambling-advertising review reference

**Operational snapshot:** 06.08.2026. Verify on official sources before a new campaign.

## Primary official sources

- Law of Ukraine “On Advertising”, Article 22-1:
  https://data.rada.gov.ua/ogd/zak/perv/text/d12889.htm
- PlayCity, illegal gambling advertising:
  https://playcity.gov.ua/nezakonna-reklama-azartnykh-ihor
- PlayCity, responsible gaming:
  https://playcity.gov.ua/vidpovidalna-hra
- PlayCity, legal basis:
  https://playcity.gov.ua/pravovi-zasady

## Required checks

- advertising is in a legally permitted channel and directed to 21+;
- advertiser is licensed for the relevant gambling activity;
- cross-brand advertising relationship is permitted and confirmed by Legal;
- information is truthful and not false, deceptive or sham;
- no claim implies easy winning, income, work alternative or quick enrichment;
- no prohibited targeting, persons, characters or war/patriotic theme;
- no free bonus instead of placing a bet and no incentive contingent on losing;
- chance, maximum (`до`), guarantee, deposit, verification, wager, time and eligibility are explicit;
- license number, issue date and issuing authority are current for the advertiser;
- exact responsible-gaming warning is present and visually compliant.

## Exact mandated warning

`Участь в азартних іграх може викликати ігрову залежність. Дотримуйтеся правил (принципів) відповідальної гри`

Закон вимагає, щоб текстове попередження займало щонайменше 15% повної площі реклами, чорним текстом на
a white background. Legal must define the advertising unit and approve the layout.

## Output discipline

- Treat every rewrite as a candidate until Legal approves it.
- Do not issue legal advice or self-approve.
- Report visual, video, alt, metadata and CTA risks, not body copy only.
- Tone of Voice can change editable phrasing, never product/legal truth.
