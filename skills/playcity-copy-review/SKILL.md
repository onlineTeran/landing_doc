# PlayCity Copy Review

Keep product truth, legal requirements, campaign copy and brand Tone of Voice separate. Identify risks and
prepare an evidence-based review; never represent the output as final legal approval.

## Load the reference

Read [rules.md](references/rules.md) for every audit or drafting task. If working inside the methodology
repository, also read `PLAYCITY-COPYWRITING-RULES.md`, the active Claims Matrix and selected product ToV.

Because regulations change, verify the official sources listed in the reference for a new campaign or
when the last verification date is unknown. Legal/compliance remains the approval owner.

## Establish placement before copy

Collect or mark missing:

- licensed advertiser and advertised brand;
- host, destination and exact CTA route;
- same-brand, same-group cross-brand or third-party relationship;
- channel/placement and 21+ targeting/access control;
- product mechanic source and current license source;
- Legal's definition of the advertising unit for warning treatment.

Treat an unresolved cross-brand relationship, unlicensed advertiser, unverified channel or P0 mechanic as
a blocker. Do not fix it with safer-sounding prose.

## Build the truth layer

Для кожної пропозиції фіксуйте дію, вибір користувача, винагороду, eligibility, умови, wager, час і джерело.
Створіть або оновіть рядки Claims Matrix з точним copy, editability, status, source/version, owner і
placement. Separate `APPROVED VERBATIM` from `APPROVED EDITABLE`.

Ніколи не виводьте актуальне число чи умову з візуалу попередньої кампанії. Не застосовуйте Tone of Voice до
verbatim legal text.

## Audit every message surface

Review:

1. hero and section copy;
2. CTA labels and destination;
3. visual concepts, characters and embedded image/video text;
4. alt text, title/meta/OG and social preview;
5. mandatory warning, license and rules/date block;
6. mobile presentation and qualifier readability.

Check easy-win, income, enrichment, guarantee, vulnerability, age, prohibited-person/theme, free-bonus,
loss-incentive and truthfulness risks. Check whether `до`, chance, deposit, verification, wager and time
are made explicit.

## Produce a review, not a verdict

Return:

| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| BLOCKER | cannot proceed without Legal/product evidence or prohibited-risk resolution |
| MAJOR | likely misleading/incomplete or mandatory element absent |
| MINOR | clarity/readability/versioning issue that does not change the mechanic |
| NOTE | optional improvement or owner question |

Для кожного finding додайте точний surface/copy, причину, офіційне правило-підтвердження, стратегію виправлення й
owner. Proposed replacement text must be labelled `CANDIDATE — LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED` unless it already
has an approved claim ID.

End with:

- placement status;
- mandatory-block status;
- claim IDs reviewed;
- open blockers;
- exact next approval needed.

Не встановлюйте `LEGAL APPROVED` самостійно.
